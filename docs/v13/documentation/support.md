heading: Support
---

The GOV.UK Prototype Kit is currently maintained by the [GOV.UK Design System team](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/contact/), who can only provide [limited support](https://github.com/alphagov/govuk-prototype-kit/issues/2389).

If you’ve got a question, idea or suggestion, share with the community of users on the [#govuk-prototype-kit](https://ukgovernmentdigital.slack.com/archives/C0647LW4R) channel on cross-government Slack.

**We cannot give general advice to the public. We do not have access to information about you held by government departments.**

For other enquiries, use the [contact form on GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/contact).
